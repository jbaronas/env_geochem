Part 1

Carbon and energy

Uses the lecture: Organic compounds, Photosynthesis and respiration, Oxidants and reductants, and The van Krevelen plot

The oxidation state of an atom counts its electrons as if every bond were ionic: the shared electrons are counted with the more electronegative atom. In organic compounds, H is +1 and O is −2, and the oxidation states of all the atoms add up to the charge of the molecule (zero for a neutral one). When there are several C atoms, give their average.

Worked example: The oxidation state of C in acetaldehyde, C₂H₄O (made when ethanol is oxidised)
  1. H: 4 × (+1) = +4.
  2. O: 1 × (−2) = −2.
  3. H and O together: +4 − 2 = +2.
  4. The molecule is neutral, so the two C together are −2.
  5. Each C, on average: −2 ÷ 2 = −1.

For an ion, the total is its charge instead of zero.

The energy of photosynthesis

As in Week 2: ΔG° of a reaction = Σ (coefficient × ΔG°f) of the products − Σ (coefficient × ΔG°f) of the reactants. Here, for glucose, the product of photosynthesis.

Worked example: ΔG° for a different reaction: methane burned in air

CH₄(g) + 2 O₂(g) → CO₂(g) + 2 H₂O(l)

  1. Products: 1 × (−394.4) + 2 × (−237.2) = −868.8 kJ.
  2. Reactants: 1 × (−50.8) + 2 × 0 = −50.8 kJ.
  3. ΔG° = −868.8 − (−50.8) = −818.0 kJ per mol of CH₄. ΔG° < 0: it can react, and releases energy.

Glossary

TermMeaning
Organic compoundA compound with C–H bonds; its carbon has an oxidation state between −4 and +4.
Oxidant, reductantThe electron acceptor (reduced in the reaction) and the electron donor (oxidised).
RespirationOrganic matter oxidised to CO₂, releasing energy; with O₂ (aerobic) or other oxidants (anaerobic).
van Krevelen plotH/C against O/C (atomic ratios): where groups of compounds plot, and how reactions move them.
DOCDissolved organic carbon: organic matter that passes a 0.45 µm filter.
Redox ladderThe order in which oxidants are used: O₂, NO₃⁻, MnO₂, Fe(OH)₃, SO₄²⁻, then methanogenesis.
DiagenesisChanges in a sediment after it is deposited.
KerogenThe insoluble organic matter in sedimentary rocks.
